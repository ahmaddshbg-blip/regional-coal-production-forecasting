# Data Attribution and Reuse

## Source

This project uses data published by the U.S. Department of Labor, Mine Safety
and Health Administration (MSHA):

- Operator Employment and Production Data Set, Quarterly;
- Mines Data Set;
- source snapshot acquired 2026-09-20; and
- coverage observed in the snapshot: 2000Q1 through 2026Q2.

Source portal:
https://arlweb.msha.gov/OpenGovernmentData/OGIMSHA.asp

Data.gov record:
https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/msha-operator-employment-and-production-data-set-quarterly

Suggested acknowledgment:

> Source data: U.S. Department of Labor, Mine Safety and Health Administration,
> Operator Employment and Production Data Set (Quarterly) and Mines Data Set,
> snapshot acquired September 20, 2026.

## Reuse

The Data.gov record classifies the quarterly dataset with public access. The
U.S. Department of Labor states that information created by DOL and presented
on its website is generally considered public domain and may be reproduced,
published, or otherwise used without permission unless otherwise noted.
Attribution is encouraged.

DOL policy:
https://beta.dol.gov/about/website-content-and-publication-practices

This project does not use DOL or MSHA seals or logos and does not imply agency
endorsement. Any third-party material separately marked as copyrighted would
require its own permission; none is intentionally redistributed here.

## Repository data policy

The raw TXT files are not committed because they total approximately 301 MB and
MSHA publishes replacement snapshots. The repository documents official
download URLs, expected filenames, acquisition date, schema, byte sizes, and
SHA-256 hashes so users can acquire and validate the same inputs where the
source remains available.

Derived fields, quality flags, forecasts, metrics, and figures produced by this
project must not be represented as MSHA-provided classifications or findings.
